01 / ABOUT THE ARTIST

THE ARTIST

Subodh
Sharma

An artist drawn to the extraordinary detail hidden within ordinary moments of nature.

Subodh Sharma is a contemporary artist specializing in hyperrealistic and photorealistic landscapes, seascapes and nature-inspired scenes. Working primarily with oil and acrylic colours, he explores light, texture, depth, reflections and atmosphere with meticulous attention.

His paintings seek to go beyond simply reproducing what a camera sees. Every brushstroke is an attempt to preserve the feeling of a place—the warmth of fading light, the movement of water, the stillness of a landscape and the subtle changes that make a moment memorable.

03 / ARTISTIC VISION

MY APPROACH

“A painting should not only show what the eye sees, but also capture what the heart feels.”

For Subodh, realism is not only about accuracy. It is about atmosphere, emotion and the relationship between light and form. His practice is an ongoing study of nature—observing its smallest details and translating them into timeless visual experiences.

04 / THE PRACTICE
01

Observe

Studying light, composition, texture and the character of each scene.

02

Build

Layering oil or acrylic colour to develop depth, form and natural atmosphere.

03

Refine

Carefully resolving reflections, surfaces and fine details until the scene feels alive.
